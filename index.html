Research

My PhD treats generation as a problem of operator design. Langevin dynamics and diffusion give stable, local, geometry-aware updates, but they are slow. Attention is fast and global, but it has no built-in notion of locality, damping or spectral structure, and on graphs it tends to blur everything together at depth.

I work on putting that structure back into attention and message passing, using tools from spectral graph theory, operator theory and dynamical systems.

Candidacy exam

Generative Modeling as Operator Design: From Diffusion to Attention

Traces generative modeling from Markov chains and Langevin dynamics through score-based diffusion to attention, and argues for attention operators with geometric and spectral structure built in.

Current projects

    PDLG: nonreversible Langevin sampling

    Faster, higher-quality image sampling for diffusion models by going beyond reversible Langevin dynamics. From the paper Beyond Reversibility: Skew-Score Corrections for Efficient Diffusion Image Sampling.

  2. In revisionGenerative models · AI for materials

    Grammar-constrained MXene generation

    With Drexel Chemical Engineering. A grammar-constrained generative model that proposes new layer-resolved MXene compositions and steers them toward chemically valid candidates.

  3. In progressGraph transformers · Spectral graph theory · Stability

    D2HGT: Damped Diffused Harmonic Graph Transformer

    A graph transformer designed to stay expressive and train stably at depth, instead of blurring node representations together. It grows out of my candidacy work and is being benchmarked against standard graph transformers, including on large graphs.

  4. In progressTransformers · Positional encoding

    Fourier RoFormer: frequency-aware attention

    Frequency-aware positional encoding for transformers, for richer context modeling in vision models.

  5. In progressGraph neural networks · Dynamical systems

    Adaptive Oscillatory GNNs for Deep Geometric Learning

    Graph neural networks built on oscillator dynamics, aimed at deep message passing with theoretical guarantees.
